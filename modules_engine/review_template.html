    How to review

    • Drag a topic onto another module, or click a topic and then click a module.
    • Drop a topic on the dashed card to start a new module.
    • Modules you change are locked automatically; lock or unlock any module with its button.
    • Hover a topic to see what it requires (blue) and what requires it (orange).
    • “Save” writes the review (module names, locks and topics) to a YAML file and “Open” loads one; keep it as review.yml in the run folder so that review-modules.sh reopens it.
    • “Re-solve” shows the command that keeps the locked modules and re-solves the rest.
